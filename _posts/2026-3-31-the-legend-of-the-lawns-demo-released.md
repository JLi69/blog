![screenshot](/blog/images/lotl_demo_screenshot.jpg)

After months of development, I am pleased to announce that *The Legend of the Lawns*
now has a public demo that anyone can check out! It feels good to finally get this
far and be able to actually officially share what the game looks like with the
world - there's still a lot more to do but I'm still happy to have reached this
milestone.

If this sounds interesting, you can check out the game on 
[itch.io](https://nullptr-error.itch.io/the-legend-of-the-lawns-demo).

I have also posted a [gameplay video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nradqkfcZHE) 
on YouTube.

Now that a public demo is out, the next steps are likely to continue adding more
lawns to the game and also start putting the story together - ideally if I
consistently work on this over the next couple of months the game can be
completed by Summer 2026 (my current goal is to get this released around July
but I'm not going to make any promises). I also kind of want to take a short
break from working on LotL for a bit and just listen to what some people are
saying about the game - feedback would be good and it would be nice to be able
to gather and address it.

Anyway, that's it for now, have a nice day!
