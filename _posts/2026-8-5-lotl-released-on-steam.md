After about of a month's worth of work and waiting, *The Legend of the Lawns*
is now finally available for download on Steam!

It's free so you can simply just download it and start playing right away.

Check out the Steam page 
[here](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4970680/The_Legend_of_the_Lawns).

This is my first game that I've ever published on Steam so I'm quite excited.
I'm not sure how popular it will end up being but I'm just glad I finally have
it published. I'm considering a couple of potential small updates in the future
such as adding support for Steam cloud saves, achievements, and making it
playable on the Steam Deck though that might be a later project.

As for other things that I'm working on, I am starting work on a new project
so I'll probably post about it on this blog sometime soon-ish.

That's it, have a nice day!
