My university has a game development club and of course, since I like to
make games, I'm a member. A while back they joined a larger network of game
development clubs from other universities across the country which they're calling
"Game Dev Club Club". Recently, they held a game jam which they called the
"Club Club Jam Jam" and I decided to participate. 

The theme was "Shell" so the idea that I came up with was to have a game in which 
the player controls a Nautilus and has to ram into enemies (predatory fish,
jellyfish) with their shell. The faster they ram into an enemy, the more damage
they do. It's a simple game but I like how it turned out - I feel that this
might be the most polished game I've ever submitted to a game jam yet.

If this sounds interesting, you can check it out on 
[itch.io](https://nullptr-error.itch.io/naughty-nautilus) and
browse the source code on [GitHub](https://github.com/JLi69/naughty-nautilus).

That's it for now, have a nice day!
